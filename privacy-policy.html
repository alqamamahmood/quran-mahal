Quran Mahal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Quran Mahal ("we", "our", "the app") is an Islamic media application providing audio bayans, video bayans, books, risalas, and photos for educational and spiritual purposes.

2. Information We Collect

• Account Information: email address when the admin logs in. Regular users are NOT required to create an account or log in.
• Device Identifier: a Firebase push-notification token, used only to deliver notifications.
• Search Words: words typed in Quran search, sent to AlQuran Cloud to return search results.
• Media Files: photos, videos and audio uploaded by the admin for the app's content; downloaded content is saved to your device.
• We do not collect analytics. We do not collect your location — prayer times and Qibla use your device's location on your device only and it is never sent to any server.

3. How We Use Your Information

• To provide app features.
• To manage admin access.
• To deliver notifications.
• To improve performance.

4. Data Storage

Account data stored securely on our servers. Downloaded files stored locally on your device.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app: Firebase (Google) for push notifications, AlQuran Cloud for Quran search results, Supabase for admin login, and Cloudflare for admin media storage — and only as required by law otherwise.

6. Advertisements

The app currently shows no advertisements.

7. No Login Required

Regular users are NOT required to create an account or log in to use the app. All content (videos, audio, books, photos) is freely accessible without any login or signup.

8. Permissions

• Storage: save downloads.
• Camera: record video/photos (admin only).
• Microphone: record audio (admin only).

9. Children's Privacy

Suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information (name, email, address) from children under 13. Push-notification tokens are device identifiers used only to deliver notifications.

Request account or data deletion by emailing hanzalamahmoodsiddiqi2@gmail.com. Delete downloaded files from your device.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes reflected in the app.
