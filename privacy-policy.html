Quran Mahal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Quran Mahal ("we", "our", "the app") is an Islamic media application providing audio bayans, video bayans, books, risalas, and photos for educational and spiritual purposes.

2. Information We Collect

• Account Information: email address when you log in.
• Usage Data: anonymous statistics to improve the app.
• Media Files: downloaded content saved to your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

• To provide app features.
• To manage your account.
• To improve performance.

4. Data Storage

Account data stored securely on our servers. Downloaded files stored locally on your device.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law.

6. Permissions

• Storage: save downloads.
• Camera: record video/photos (admin only).
• Microphone: record audio (admin only).

7. Children's Privacy

Suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

Request account deletion by contacting us. Delete downloaded files from your device.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes reflected in the app.
